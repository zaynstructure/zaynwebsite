ripple

A touch-reveal piece: ripples of light peel back a hidden video loop, each one paired with a note from a small generative synth tuned to a shifting scale.

Runs in its own frame — nothing loads until you tap it. Once open, tap inside it to reveal and to hear it; sound needs that first tap to start, and silent mode should be off on mobile.
