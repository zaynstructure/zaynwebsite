the ghost dance

Love as a coupled two-body loop. Two bodies move toward calm or away from it, but neither can see the other directly — each answers only the other's trace from a moment ago. Settle them and rings of devotion carve into the room where they rest; erode the attraction and the bond breaks as romantic even while everything else holds.

Runs in its own frame and nothing loads until you click it. Try the presets: settling, pursue–withdraw, fading spark, co-escalation. Time × speeds up the slow layer so the rings form sooner. Part of the epg work.
