drift home

What goes unnamed does not stay where it happened. An event lands in a field of filtered noise and drifts, from texture toward regime toward identity. Tap to name it: the name holds its pitch as a red line and a sine tone, while what it named keeps moving away from it.

A sound piece: runs in its own frame, nothing loads until you click, and audio starts on its own start button. Tap inside to name, press event (or e) for a new one. Part of the epg work.
