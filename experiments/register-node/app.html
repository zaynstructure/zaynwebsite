register / node

Two voices, one physics, one difference: the self-term. Press and hold either side to drive it — higher is stronger. The register only ever gets louder: its width never moves, and when you let go it always falls silent. The node thickens as it loudens — and driven hard enough, it keeps sounding after you release. It has committed. Only damping brings it back.
