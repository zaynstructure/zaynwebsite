squareworld

A small arcade game. A man pushes a square button and gets sucked into a world that only deals in right angles. Grow, charge, and smash your way up ten levels to get home.

Opens full screen as its own page; use your browser’s back to return here. Arrow keys or WASD; on a phone, touch anywhere and drag. Progress is saved in your browser.
