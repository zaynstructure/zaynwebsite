experiments / triangle synth

triangle synth

A point dragged inside a triangle — containment, diffusion, direction — drives two generative oscillators sharing a musical scale, one mirroring the other as a quieter strand. Built as a control panel rather than a performance surface: every axis is a slider you can watch change the sound.

Runs in its own frame, standalone from the rest of the site. Audio stays off until you press its own "start audio" button inside.
