bio / statement

Zayn Wiwchar is a media artist based on Vancouver Island, on Lekwungen territory. His work spans animation, sound-responsive video projection, video, and collage, made through layering and repeated editing.

Pieces typically start from field recordings, found footage, or physical fragments, then get reworked through cycles of editing and recomposition.

He has a background in social work, psychology, and mental health.
