How CircleShare works

A simple way for neighbours to share tools, gear, skills, and essentials through small, self-organized circles.

What is CircleShare?

CircleShare is a neighbourhood-focused sharing network. You join circles based on interests — like Photography, Tools, Sewing, Kitchen — and borrow or lend within those circles. It’s mutual aid, not a marketplace.

Why circles?

Circles keep sharing small, manageable, and community-driven. Each circle builds its own norms, care steps, and expectations. This keeps things fair without needing heavy rules.

What you can share
  • tools and repair gear
  • cameras, lenses, tripods
  • kitchen appliances and cookware
  • sewing machines, textile tools
  • camping or outdoor gear
  • clothing and basics (optional)
  • food and essentials (free section)
How borrowing works
  • Browse items in your circle.
  • Complete a tiny “care step” (30–60 sec) if required.
  • Request the item. Lender confirms pickup.
  • Pickup at a porch bin or public place.
  • Return in the same condition.
How lending works
  • Add your item and choose a circle.
  • Set a pickup method (porch bin / meet).
  • Borrowers complete the care step you choose.
  • You decide whether to approve each request.
Care steps

Care steps are small, simple actions (often under a minute) that show someone understands the basics — e.g. how to cap a lens, how to load a sewing bobbin, or how to store a drill safely. They keep things fair and reduce misuse without gatekeeping.

FAQ
Do I need an account?

For lending and borrowing, yes. For the free food & clothing page, no account is needed.

What if someone doesn’t return an item?

Circles handle this with small norms. Optionally, lenders can require a deposit, or the Shared Care Pool can cover replacements for those who need support.

Why intersections instead of exact addresses?

Safety. Borrowers only see full address after approval. Most pickups happen by a porch bin or in public places anyway.

Is everything free?

Yes. This is a mutual-aid tool, not a marketplace. You don’t pay to borrow items. The free page is completely without requirements.

What if something breaks?

It happens. Items have clear care expectations. If something breaks despite normal use, it’s usually treated as shared risk. Deposits are optional and never required for users who can’t afford them.

Who runs CircleShare?

It’s structured to be community-led: circles self-organize norms, and the platform stays lightweight and simple. No ads, no selling of data, no algorithmic feed.
