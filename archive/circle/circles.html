How circles work

Neighbourhood Circles
Connect people who live nearby. Ideal for sharing everyday items with simple norms of care and return.

Private Circles
Create a small, invite-only group—friends, neighbours, an apartment building, or coworkers. Fully self-organized with your own shared expectations.
