Community Pools

Optional small contributions that help keep the system fair, accessible, and stable — and support neighbours when things go wrong.

General Pool

A shared fund used across the whole community. It helps cover small replacements, deposits for people who need support, and occasional repairs. It is not required and is always transparently tracked.

  • Helps borrowers who can’t afford first-time deposits
  • Supports low-income or emergency needs
  • Backs up neighbourhood pools when they fall short
  • Used for approved categories only (repairs, replacements, access)
Contribute

Neighbourhood Pools

Each neighbourhood circle maintains its own small “micro-pool.” These funds are used locally for quick repairs, replacing small shared items, and supporting first-time borrowers inside that circle.

  • Funds stay inside your neighbourhood
  • Used first before touching the general pool
  • Helps maintain shared assets (like a ladder or lawnmower)
  • Decisions made by neighbourhood members
View neighbourhood circles

Why Two Pools?

This model balances fairness and trust. Local funds keep neighbourhoods empowered and quick, while the general pool ensures no one is left behind.

How a request works:

  1. Neighbourhood pool is checked first.
  2. If it doesn’t fully cover the request,
  3. General pool may top it up (within limits).

This keeps everything sustainable while still supporting people who need it.
