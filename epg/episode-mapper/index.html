epg / episode mapper

episode mapper

Logs a single emotional episode across five axes — charge/intensity, speed of onset, coherence, movement since it began, and whether it returned — plus which channels (body, thought, action) it recruited. Reflects the entry back as an interpretation and a shape.

This is a working tool, not a demo video — it runs standalone, outside the site's own styling.
