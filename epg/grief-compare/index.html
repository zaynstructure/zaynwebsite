epg / grief: blocked vs completing

grief: blocked vs completing

The same loss attractor and the same wave pattern, run side by side. In one, cognition pulls back each time the wave peaks, the field stays sealed, and the wave returns. In the other, feeling is allowed to spread and find expression, the basin flattens, and the episode passes. What differs isn't how much it hurts — it's how the activation is organized.

A storyboard, not a simulation: six stages per side, played in step. Press play, or click along the bar to jump. The field is activation, dashed rings are the channels it recruits (body, feeling, cognition, behavior), and the triangle is regulation — containment, diffusion, direction.
