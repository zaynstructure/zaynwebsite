epg / field shader

field shader

Emotion as a multi-parameter system, rendered as a surface with particles moving across it. Damping is how fast a signal returns to baseline; coupling, how much outside input moves you; noise, the background interference; rhythm, how steady the cycles are; gradient, the pull of intent; attractor, the dominance of one theme; coherence, how well it all aligns.

Runs in its own frame and nothing loads until you click. Presets A–D load typical field patterns, each with a short description; the sliders move every parameter live.
