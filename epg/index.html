epg

emotional process geometry

Emotional Process Geometry (EPG) is a project I have been developing over the last few years. It comes out of my work in an anxiety program, my own art practice, and a combination of deep introspection and contemplative practice on my own emotional experience. It tries to model emotional activation as something that moves over time rather than a set of fixed states. Instead of sorting a feeling into a category, it follows an episode as it forms, builds, and travels, and whether it settles, returns, or holds.

I approach this through dynamical systems. An episode is traced through a structured field, described by qualities like its charge, how quickly it comes on, how coherent it is, how far it has moved since onset, and whether it tends to recur. Over time the model keeps returning to a handful of processes I find myself watching for in the work and in people: entrenchment, pattern breaking, reinforcement, collapse, and suppression.

I do not claim to be a mathematician; I rely on my own understanding, along with current research in psychology, affective science, and dynamical systems, to inform the approach. Parts of the model are more formal than what I show here, and I have kept those to myself for now. What is on this page is a way to meet the system by using it, and a start at representing that activation spatially, through sound and image.
