epg / expressive field

expressive field

Activation as a field rather than a point. Diffusion spreads it, damping pulls it back to rest, an attractor draws it toward one theme, drift pushes it in a direction, and rhythm makes it breathe. Trigger an event to disturb it and watch how it organizes — then resolve the event and watch it return, or not.

Runs in its own frame and nothing loads until you click. The readout above the field tracks the step size, coherence (spatial, temporal, harmonic) and drift. View switches colour and grayscale; flow switches expressive and neutral. Needs WebGL 2.
