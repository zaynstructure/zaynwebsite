The Pool

The Community Pool is a shared fund and item reserve that helps cover small costs, replace lost or broken items, and support members who can’t afford deposits. Everyone can contribute. Everyone can benefit.

How it Works

  • A shared pot of small contributions from members.
  • Used to subsidize deposits, repairs, or replacements.
  • Transparent and capped — the pool is not for profit.
  • Decisions follow simple rules, not voting or drama.

Why It Exists

  • To make borrowing easier for people with fewer resources.
  • To protect lenders from occasional losses.
  • To prevent the system from needing strict deposits or ID checks.
  • To build trust without demanding perfection from anyone.
