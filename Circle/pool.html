Community Pool

A shared community fund used for:

  • • Repairing shared items
  • • Replacing lost/damaged items
  • • Covering borrowing deposits for members who need support
  • • Supporting small mutual-aid requests

How it Works

Participation is optional. Contributions are pooled together and used only for shared community benefit. All spending and monthly usage summaries are posted transparently.

Members can propose repairs or replacements, and the community votes on items above a certain threshold.
