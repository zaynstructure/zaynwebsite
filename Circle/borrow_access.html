Borrowing Access

Your borrowing access grows naturally as you participate in the community. There are no hard requirements — just cooperative reciprocity.

New Member

  • Borrow 1 item at a time
  • Low-risk items available
  • Simple accountability step for first 3 borrows

Active Member

  • Borrow 2 items at a time
  • Medium-risk items unlocked
  • Deposit support often waived

Reached by: lending 1 item, joining a Circle, contributing to the Pool, or 2+ successful returns.

Community Member

  • Borrow 3 items at a time
  • All-risk items unlocked
  • Deposit support always waived

Reached by: sharing several items, circle involvement, consistent returns, or contributing to repairs & accessibility.

Borrowing access is never used to block essential needs. It simply grows as you participate.
