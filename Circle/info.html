How CircleShare Works

CircleShare is a simple community library. Borrow what you need. Lend what you choose. Free section available to everyone.

1. Borrowing

Anyone can browse the general library. For your first 3 borrows, you choose one small accountability step (deposit, care step, backup contact, or joining a circle). After that, borrowing becomes easier.

2. Lending

Lenders choose what to share and set their own conditions. You can always decline requests. High-value items can require membership in a circle.

3. Circles

Circles are small interest groups (Photography, Tools, Cooking, etc.). They help teach care basics, set norms, and reduce risk for delicate gear. Joining a circle unlocks more items in that category.

4. Free Food & Clothing

No requirements. Take what you need or share what you can. No borrowing process. No returns.

5. Safety & Trust

We avoid IDs and proof of address. Instead, we use small, human-centered safeguards: short loan windows, light care steps, and circle norms. These keep the community safe without creating barriers.

FAQ

Do I need to pay to join?

No. CircleShare is free. Some items may require a tiny returnable deposit for first-time borrowers.

What if someone doesn’t return an item?

Most issues resolve through messaging. Lenders can choose safeties like deposits or circle requirements. High-value items always have extra layers.

Why circles?

They protect delicate gear, teach care basics, and build trust. They’re small, friendly, and open to anyone.

Is my address public?

No. Borrowers only see a nearby intersection until the request is approved.

Can I donate food or clothing?

Yes. The free section is open to everyone with zero conditions.
