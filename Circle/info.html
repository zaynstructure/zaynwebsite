How CircleShare Works

A simple, community-powered way to borrow, share, and help each other. Three parts: Borrow/Lend, Give/Receive, and Circles.

Borrow / Lend

The Community Library. Neighbours lend items like tools, appliances, gear, and more. Borrow with a simple calendar system and clear return expectations.

  • Browse items and open lending options
  • First 3 borrows use a small accountability step
  • Afterward, borrowing becomes easier
  • Lenders choose who can borrow based on fairness settings

Give / Receive

Free Food & Clothing section — no requirements, no borrowing process. Just take what you need or share what you can.

  • Free items only (food, clothing, essentials)
  • No returns or conditions
  • Quick “Claim” button for pickup

Circles

Small trust-based groups that unlock access to more items and shared expectations. Three types:

  • Neighbourhood Circles: local, proximity-based
  • Interest Circles: skill-specific groups
  • Private Circles: invite-only friend groups

Community Pool

A small optional fund that supports deposits for people who can’t afford them, helps repair shared items, and covers light platform costs.

  • Always optional
  • Used only to support fairness and access
  • Transparent usage and simple rules

Fairness & Safety

CircleShare is designed to offer access without feeling punitive. Fairness comes from small steps, not barriers.

  • First-three-borrows system prevents abuse
  • Lenders choose simple settings: Open, Participating, Trusted
  • Borrowers build trust through returns, lending, or circle basics
  • Lenders can decline any borrow request

FAQ

Do I need to join a circle?
No. Circles are optional and only unlock extra items.

Is there any cost?
No. Borrowing is free. A tiny optional pool exists for shared support.

What if someone doesn’t return something?
The first-three-borrows system protects lenders. Most risky behaviour happens at the start, so early steps prevent issues later.

Can I create a private group?
Yes. Private Circles allow small friend or neighbour groups to share just with each other.

Why is the free section separate?
Free items don’t have conditions or return steps. This keeps mutual aid simple and consistent.
