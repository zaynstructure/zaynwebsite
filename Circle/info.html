How CircleShare Works

A simple sharing network built around trust, care, and local community.

Borrowing

Borrow items shared by your neighbours or circle members.

  • No renting, no selling — just sharing.
  • Your first 3 borrows include a small accountability step.
  • After good returns, borrowing gets easier.

Circles

Small groups that make sharing easier and more trusted.

  • Building Circles: neighbours in your building/block
  • Interest Circles: photography, tools, cooking, etc.
  • Private Circles: invite-only groups you create

Free Food & Clothing

A simple page where anyone can offer or pick up free essentials.

  • No borrowing rules
  • No signup required to browse
  • Just take what you need or share what you can

Community Pool

An optional shared fund for repairs, replacements, and access support.

  • Supports people who can’t afford deposits
  • Helps repair or replace shared items
  • Entirely voluntary — no fees, no pressure
