About CircleShare

CircleShare is a simple, community-led sharing network. It helps neighbours lend tools, borrow gear, offer essentials, and build small circles of mutual support.

Our mission

To make sharing effortless, warm, and accessible — reducing waste, saving money, and strengthening community ties without relying on profit-driven models.

What we believe
Sharing over buying

Most people own tools and gear they rarely use. Sharing makes life easier and reduces unnecessary consumption.

Community over platforms

CircleShare grows through small circles that manage themselves. The platform exists only to support, not control.

Mutual care, not gatekeeping

Simple “care steps” keep items safe without creating barriers. Everyone is welcome; no one is judged by their income.

Free access to essentials

The Free Food & Clothing page is open to all. No account, no requirements — just help when it’s needed most.

Privacy and safety first

Intersections protect your address. Borrowers only receive details after approval, and porch pickups or public locations keep interactions light and safe.

Who runs this?

CircleShare is structured to be community-led. Circles build their own norms, and the platform stays simple, transparent, and free from ads or selling personal data.
