Circles

Circles are small trust-based groups that share knowledge, expectations, and lending care basics. Joining a circle unlocks more items and helps build connection.

Neighbourhood Circles

Local clusters based on rough proximity. Meet neighbours, share everyday items, and build micro-trust networks.

View neighbourhood circles

Interest Circles

Skill-based or theme-based groups (Photography, DIY Tools, Camping, Cooking, Cycling, etc.). Each circle has simple care basics and optional micro-trainings.

Explore interest circles

Private Circles

Create a closed group with friends, roommates, coworkers, or your apartment building. Your items stay visible only to your group.
