Circles

Circles are small groups where sharing feels easier and more trusting. You can connect by building, by interest, or with a private group you already know.

Building Circles

For the people in your building or on your block. Share everyday items, small favors, and free corner things with neighbours.

  • Join by address or building code
  • Hyperlocal borrowing and free items
  • Low friction, low risk, very simple
Join your building circle

Interest Circles

For skills, tools, and hobbies that need a bit more care. Photography, sewing, camping, tools, music gear, and more.

  • Simple care basics before borrowing
  • Shared guidelines for higher-risk items
  • Fair borrowing requirements, not wealth-based
Browse interest circles

Private Circles

For small invite-only groups: friends, collectives, pods, project teams, or anyone who already trusts each other and wants to organize together.

  • Invite-only sharing and pooling
  • Set your own norms together
  • Good for shared projects or mutual aid pods
Create or join a private circle
