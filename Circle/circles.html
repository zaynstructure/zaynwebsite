Circles

Circles are small interest groups that help people share specialized items more safely and confidently. You don’t need to join a circle to start borrowing from the general library — but circles unlock more items and teach simple care basics.

How circles work

  • You browse available circles in your area.
  • Each circle has a few simple care basics (20–60 sec steps).
  • Joining a circle unlocks more items (cameras, tools, kitchen gear, etc.).
  • High-value items may require circle membership.
  • Circles help build trust without barriers or ID requirements.

Joining is optional, open to everyone, and takes less than a minute.
