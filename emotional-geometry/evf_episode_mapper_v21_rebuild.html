About v2.1

This app does not map an emotion directly. It maps a bounded activation episode as a temporal movement signature, then compares a generated read with the user’s instinctive shape-word.

Body, thought, and urge/action are treated as recruitment channels. Felt texture is collected separately as lived quality.

feeling is lived · emotion is patterned · field is mapped

Data is stored only in this browser’s local storage unless exported.
